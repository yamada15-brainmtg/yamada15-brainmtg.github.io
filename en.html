Meeting overview

Dates February 8 (Mon)–10 (Wed), 2027
Venue ISOLA Izu-Kogen (tentative)
Capacity Approximately 50–70 participants at the tentative venue. The venue may change depending on the number of participants.
Format Three-day residential meeting (two nights)

About the meeting

The meeting will discuss brain development, circuit formation, and evolution from diverse perspectives, focusing on fundamental principles of brain construction conserved across species and the evolutionary plasticity that gave rise to higher brain functions.

We aim to bring together studies of diverse animal species and to discuss what has been conserved across species, what changed during evolution, and how these features contributed to higher brain functions. Early-career researchers are especially encouraged to share unpublished data and ongoing research ideas for open discussion.

Format

  • Participants are generally expected to attend the full meeting.
  • The estimated fee is JPY 35,000–40,000, including the meeting fee, two nights of accommodation, and all meals. This estimate is subject to change.
  • Accommodation is generally planned as four participants per room.
  • Poster presentations and short talks are planned to provide presentation opportunities for early-career researchers and students.
  • Partial travel support for students may be available depending on the budget. Details will be announced later.
